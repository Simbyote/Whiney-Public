# Code of conduct

Whiney is a small project made by and for people who play Skyrim together.
Keep it a place where anyone can ask a question, report a problem or send a
fix without being made to feel unwelcome.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to modding or to
  open source.
- Keep criticism about the work, specific and constructive.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Credit other people's work, including mod authors'.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sharing mods or other content against their authors' permissions in this
  project's spaces.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other project space. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and
temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behaviour they find
harmful.

To report a problem privately, open a private report through the
repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**) and say it is a
conduct report. The maintainers will keep the reporter's identity
confidential.

This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
